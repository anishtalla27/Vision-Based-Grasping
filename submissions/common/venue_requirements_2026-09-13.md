## The dual-submission problem

ICDM Teen notifies on 18 Sept. IEEE BigData HS Symposium forbids concurrent
submission of the same or substantially similar paper. Its deadline is 20 Sept,
two days after the ICDM decision, so the clean path is: wait for ICDM on 18 Sept,
and submit to BigData HS only if ICDM rejects. If ICDM accepts, do not send the
same paper to BigData. The REU Symposium deadline is 18 Sept, the same day as the
ICDM decision, so it is only safe if the ICDM email arrives early that day or if
the paper is a substantially different 6-10 page version. It publishes in the
same IEEE BigData proceedings, so do not send to both BigData tracks.

Yau, AIAS+ and CJSJ all allow work that is under review or submitted elsewhere,
with disclosure. See each entry.

## 1. S.-T. Yau High School Science Award, Computer Award (North America)

Sources: https://www.yau-science-awards.org/competitioncategory/computer-guide.html ,
https://www.yau-science-awards.org/north-america.html ,
https://www.yau-awards.com/en/page-rule.html , https://www.yau-awards.com/en/page-assessment.html

- Deadline: 15 Sept 2026, 23:59 UTC. Registration and all materials. Nothing accepted late.
- Portal: https://www.yau-science-awards.org/login.html (one-time email link creates the account).
- Team: 1 to 3 students from the same school, plus 1 or 2 instructors. Solo is fine.
- Instructor: REQUIRED. Must be a teacher at a high school, university, or research institute. Cannot be from a company or a paid training center. You need a teacher's name and consent by 15 Sept.
- Report: one PDF with these nine parts in order: cover page (names, state, country, school), title, authors, abstract, keywords, table of contents, body, bibliography starting on its own page, acknowledgment. Template: Research_Report_Template.docx on the guide page. No page limit stated. English encouraged, required for the global final.
- Acknowledgment: 1-2 pages (500-1500 words). Must state each person's contribution, all outside help, and a detailed, truthful account of AI use: tool names and versions, which stages, purpose, timing and frequency. Chat records go under "Other Materials". Undeclared AI use is grounds for disqualification. This project used Claude Code heavily for code, analysis and drafting, and GPT-4o as the studied system, so this section needs care.
- Declaration of Academic Integrity: signed, uploaded.
- Plagiarism check report: required before the deadline, similarity must be under 10%. Recommended platforms are HowNet and PaperPass (Chinese). For an English report, run it through a school-available checker (Turnitin via a teacher) or PaperPass and upload the report.
- Prior submission disclosure: must state at registration that the same research is submitted to ICDM Teen (and any others you send).
- Optional but recommended: source code, executable package, and a short video of the evaluation pipeline to prove authenticity.
- Judging: relevance, significance, originality, robust results, academic norms (original content clearly separated from background), integrity. Regional round is an oral defense (1 Oct to 2 Nov); global final at Tsinghua 5-6 Dec, English defense.
- Fees: none.
- What to build: convert `paper.tex` to the Yau structure (add cover, keywords, TOC, separate bibliography, acknowledgment). Frame the contribution as the controlled three-paradigm benchmark plus System D, not the ResNet.

## 4. Columbia Junior Science Journal 2026-27

Sources: https://columbiajuniorsciencejournal.org/guidelines , https://columbiajuniorsciencejournal.org/faq

- Deadline: 30 Sept 2026. Quarterfinalists 30 Oct, semifinalists 20 Nov, finalists 11 Dec, publication 26 Mar 2027.
- Length: original research 2 to 3 pages, figures and references excluded.
- Files, named exactly: LastnameFirstname_paper.docx (their Original Research template, text plus figures plus captions inline), LastnameFirstname_figures.ppt (their Figures template, figures separately), LastnameFirstname_form.pdf (Permission to Publish form). Word only, no LaTeX.

- Fees: none mentioned.
- What to build: a 2-3 page Word version from the ICDM text. Methods in about 300 words, one accuracy figure plus failure taxonomy, discussion on the System D result.
