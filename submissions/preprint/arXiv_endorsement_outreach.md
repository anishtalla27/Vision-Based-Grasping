# arXiv endorsement outreach for cs.RO: ready-to-send emails

Prepared 19 September 2026 for Anish Talla. Endorsement code **BL7NDW**. 99 people, 32 with an address printed on their own paper, 67 without.

## Read this first

- arXiv's endorsement help page says "it is inappropriate to email large numbers of potential endorsers at once, or to repeatedly email the same endorser." Send five at a time, wait three or four days, and stop the moment one person endorses you.
- **Addresses.** Each address below was copied from the first two pages of one of that person's own arXiv papers and matched to their name. None is guessed. Before sending, open the paper link and check the address against the PDF, because a name match can be wrong for people with common names. For the people in Part 2 no address is printed on their papers, so look on their personal or lab web page. Do not guess a Gmail address for anyone.
- **The personal sentence.** The second sentence of each email is specific to that person's paper and was written from its abstract. Open the paper before you send, so you can stand behind the sentence if they reply with a question. Edit it if you see something better to say.
- **Greeting.** Senior (last) authors get "Dr.". Everyone else gets their full name, since many first authors are PhD students.
- **The paper.** It is linked, not attached. Check that the Drive link opens in a private browser window, which confirms sharing is set to "Anyone with the link".
- The email subject for everyone: `High school student requesting an arXiv endorsement for cs.RO`
